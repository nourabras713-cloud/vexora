HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

On desktop, use the Right Arrow to accelerate and the Left Arrow to brake or reverse. On a phone or tablet, use the touch controls shown in the game. Keep your car balanced over ramps and landings to reach the finish.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About Drive Mad

Drive Mad is a physics-based driving game created by Fancade. Each level challenges you to cross unusual tracks filled with ramps, gaps, moving obstacles and tricky landings. Control your acceleration and braking to keep the vehicle balanced. This VEXORA page links to the browser version on Poki.
