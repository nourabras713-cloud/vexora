HOW TO PLAY

Police Pursuit 2 Controls

Use the Up and Down arrows to drive and brake, Left and Right to steer, Space for nitro, Shift to drift, Tab to toggle the siren, and C to change the camera.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About Police Pursuit 2

Police Pursuit 2 is a 3D police chase racing game where you pursue criminals through city traffic. The game features nitro, drifting, a changing camera and mission-based police pursuits.

POLICE PURSUIT 2 GUIDE

Police Pursuit 2 Online

Police Pursuit 2 puts you behind the wheel of a police car during high-speed city pursuits. The roads are filled with traffic, so successful runs require both speed and careful steering. The objective is to locate and stop criminal vehicles without damaging your own car too much.

Nitro is useful when you need to close a gap quickly, while drifting can help you take sharper turns. The map helps you locate the targets, so learning the city layout and planning your route can make each pursuit easier.
