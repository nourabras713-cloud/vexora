HOW TO PLAY

Racing Horizon Controls

Use the Up and Down arrows to drive and brake, Left and Right to steer, N for nitro, C to change the camera and Spacebar to brake.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About Racing Horizon

Racing Horizon is a 3D traffic racing game with missions, endless driving, time challenges and police chase modes. Money earned in the game can be used for cars and upgrades.

RACING HORIZON GUIDE

Racing Horizon Online

Racing Horizon combines highway driving with several different game modes. Missions provide structured objectives, Endless mode focuses on surviving traffic, Time Challenges test your speed and Police Chase mode adds pursuit pressure.

Use nitro on clear stretches of road rather than activating it immediately in dense traffic. Keep an eye on vehicles ahead and use controlled steering to avoid collisions. Upgrading the car can improve your performance as the challenges become more difficult.

If you enjoy 3D highway racing, traffic games and police chase games, Racing Horizon is a useful addition to the VEXORA racing collection.
