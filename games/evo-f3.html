GAME-SPECIFIC GUIDE

Evo-F3 – The Airport Driving and Stunt Sandbox

Evo-F3 is the third game in Ciorbyn's Evo-F driving series. This edition moves the action to a large airport where players can explore open runways, drive sports cars and other vehicles, and create their own stunt areas by moving ramps with a forklift. It is a free-roaming driving sandbox rather than a conventional race with a fixed track and finish line.

What Makes Evo-F3 Different?

The airport setting gives the game room for high-speed runs, drifting practice and vehicle experiments. Instead of choosing one car and following a single course, you can switch between several types of vehicles, including sports cars and an airport passenger bus. The returning forklift adds another layer: use its lifting equipment to reposition ramps and objects, then drive a car through the stunt layout you have created.

Moving aircraft make the runway feel active and introduce a hazard. Watch the landing and take-off areas, because getting underneath a moving plane can damage or destroy your vehicle. The open environment encourages experimentation, but paying attention to your surroundings is still important.

Explore the Airport

Start by driving around the airfield and learning its layout. Long, open stretches are useful for testing acceleration and top speed, while the ramps and obstacles provide opportunities for jumps and controlled landings. Try a few laps around the open sections before attempting more complicated stunts. This helps you understand how each vehicle responds to steering and braking.

The airport is not just scenery. The moving planes, available vehicles and movable objects are part of the sandbox experience. You can create a route, move a ramp to a new position, and then try to clear it with a sports car. If a stunt goes wrong, use the repair or reset controls and try again.

Vehicles: Sports Cars, Bus and Forklift

Evo-F3 offers more than one driving style. Sports cars are suited to fast acceleration, high-speed runs and drifting. The passenger bus is larger and behaves differently, making it interesting to drive around the wide airport environment. The forklift is the utility vehicle that lets you interact with the stunt props and move ramps into position.

Switching vehicles changes the way you approach the same area. A sports car can take a ramp at speed, while the forklift is better suited to positioning objects carefully. Use each vehicle for the task it handles best rather than trying to make every machine behave like a racing car.

Create Custom Stunts with the Forklift

One of the game's distinctive features is the ability to move ramps and other objects with the forklift. Use its arm and fork controls to lift, lower and reposition objects. You can build a simple jump, make a longer stunt route or move obstacles to create a different challenge. After arranging the props, return to a faster vehicle and test your layout.

Start with one ramp and a clear landing area. Once you understand how the forklift responds, experiment with more complicated arrangements. Leave enough room for the car to approach and land safely. The fun comes from designing a stunt and then seeing whether your driving can complete it.

Controls and Keyboard Guide

The browser version is designed for keyboard and mouse play. WASD controls the vehicle, while the mouse is used to look around or interact. Additional keys operate the camera, repair and reset functions, slow motion, and the forklift arm.

Tips for Better Driving and Stunts

  • Learn the airport first: explore the runway and identify open areas before attempting jumps.
  • Use the right vehicle: sports cars are suited to speed, while the forklift is for moving props.
  • Build simple ramps first: test one obstacle before creating a complex stunt course.
  • Control your approach speed: a clean landing is easier when you do not hit a ramp too fast.
  • Watch moving aircraft: stay clear of active runway paths and landing zones.
  • Use slow motion: the T key can help you observe a difficult stunt or vehicle movement.
  • Repair when needed: press R after damage instead of continuing with a badly affected vehicle.
  • Change the camera: press C to find a view that makes ramps and landings easier to judge.

Is Evo-F3 a Racing Game or a Driving Simulator?

It is best described as a 3D driving sandbox with stunt and simulation elements. There are no standard championship laps that define the whole experience. Players are free to explore, test vehicles, build obstacles and create their own driving challenges. If you enjoy open-ended car games more than strict racing competitions, this format offers a different kind of challenge.
