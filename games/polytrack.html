HOW TO PLAY

PolyTrack Controls and Instructions

Use WASD or the arrow keys to drive: Up/W to accelerate, Down/S to brake or reverse, and Left/Right or A/D to steer. Press Enter or R to restart a track. In the editor, use the mouse to place and adjust track pieces; press T to test your creation.

What Is PolyTrack?

PolyTrack is a browser-based racing game created by Kodub. Its low-poly visual style keeps the focus on the track, vehicle movement and racing line. Instead of competing in a conventional pack of cars, the central challenge is to complete a course as quickly and cleanly as possible. Every corner, landing and acceleration point can affect your final time.

The game includes pre-built tracks as well as a level editor. This gives players two ways to enjoy it: race immediately on existing circuits or design custom courses with ramps, curves and other track pieces. Community-created tracks add variety and provide fresh challenges beyond the standard selection.

Best PolyTrack Tips for Faster Lap Times

  • Learn the route first: a clean finish is more useful than a fast crash while you are learning.
  • Brake before the corner: reducing speed before turning helps maintain a stable line.
  • Avoid unnecessary steering: smooth inputs preserve momentum and reduce corrections.
  • Land with the car aligned: sideways landings can cost time and make the next turn harder.
  • Use the full track width: approach bends with enough space to turn without clipping the edge.
  • Study your ghost: compare your current run with your best attempt to identify where time is lost.
  • Practice one section at a time: repeat difficult jumps or corners until the timing feels consistent.
  • Restart strategically: if a run is clearly lost, restart and apply what you learned.

What Makes PolyTrack Different from Other Car Games?

Many browser driving games focus on traffic, combat, stunts or open-world exploration. PolyTrack is more focused on precision time trials and creative track design. Players are encouraged to find the best racing line, understand vehicle handling and improve their own records. The editor adds a creative layer: instead of only mastering tracks made by someone else, you can build your own challenges and share them with the community.

PolyTrack Track Editor: Build Your Own Racing Course

The built-in editor lets you place and rotate pieces to form a route. You can create straight sections, curves, ramps and more complex sequences. Start with a simple loop and test it before adding difficult jumps or tight turns. A track that looks exciting in the editor still needs to be drivable, so test each section and adjust awkward transitions.

When your design works, you can share it with other players using the game's track-sharing features. Community tracks are a major source of replay value because they expose you to different layouts, shortcuts and driving challenges. If you are new to editing, explore existing community designs to understand how experienced builders combine pieces.

Cars, Customization and Racing Style

PolyTrack allows players to customize the appearance of their vehicle. Depending on the available version, options include paint colors and other visual details. Cosmetic changes do not replace driving skill: the fastest route still depends on braking, steering, acceleration and clean landings. Choose a look you enjoy, then focus on improving your lines and consistency.

PolyTrack vs TrackMania

PolyTrack takes inspiration from the time-trial racing style associated with TrackMania, especially its emphasis on precise driving, unusual track shapes and shaving time from a run. PolyTrack offers a lighter browser-friendly presentation and a built-in track editor. It is a separate game created by Kodub, not an official TrackMania release.
