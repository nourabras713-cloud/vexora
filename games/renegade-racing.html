About Renegade Racing

Renegade Racing is a 2D stunt racing game with multiple tracks, jumps, tricks and upgrades. The physics make balance and landing control important.

RENEGADE RACING GUIDE

Renegade Racing Online

Renegade Racing combines racing with stunt physics. Tracks contain ramps, jumps and obstacles, so simply holding the accelerator is not enough. You need to control the car in the air and land without losing momentum.

The arrow keys provide the main movement controls while Space is used for jumping. Try to keep the vehicle balanced during long jumps and make small corrections instead of sudden movements.
