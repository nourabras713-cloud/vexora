HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Player 1: press W to jump and hold E to raise your weapon; release E to shoot. Player 2: press I to jump and hold O to shoot. Knock your opponent off the rooftop. The first player to reach five points wins. Keyboard controls are recommended for desktop play.

Rooftop Snipers – The Complete Guide to the Two-Player Shooting Game

Rooftop Snipers is a fast, funny and competitive action shooting game built around a simple objective: knock your opponent off the rooftop before they knock you down. Developed by New Eich Games, the game uses a compact two-button control system and physics-based movement to create unpredictable duels. You do not need a complicated weapon menu or a long tutorial to understand the goal. Jump to avoid incoming shots, fire at the right moment and use the recoil and impact of each hit to push the other player toward the edge.

What Is Rooftop Snipers?

Rooftop Snipers is a 2D pixel-style shooting game in which two characters face each other on a narrow rooftop. Each player can jump and shoot, but positioning is limited. The challenge is to time these actions so that your opponent loses balance and falls from the platform. A successful hit can push a character backward, while a poorly timed shot can leave the shooter exposed or even move them toward danger.

The short rounds make it easy to start a quick match, while the physics create plenty of surprising moments. Characters bounce, fall and react to the force of each shot. Different environments add variety to the matches, and the simple controls make the game suitable for friendly competition on one computer.

How to Play and Win

The main goal is to score five points before your opponent. A point is earned when the rival is knocked off the rooftop. You can play against the computer or invite another person to share the keyboard. The match rewards timing more than frantic button pressing. Jumping at the right moment can avoid an incoming shot, while firing when the other character is vulnerable can create enough knockback to win the round.

Do not hold the shoot button without thinking about your position. Watch where your character is standing and consider how the recoil or impact may affect balance. A shot that connects is useful, but a shot that sends you off the edge is a mistake. Try to stay near the safer middle area until you see a clear opportunity to attack.

Tips for Better Rooftop Battles

  • Jump to dodge: Use jumps to avoid shots instead of staying in one position.
  • Time your shots: Wait for an opponent to land or move into a vulnerable position before firing.
  • Respect recoil: Your own weapon can affect your position. Avoid firing repeatedly when you are close to the edge.
  • Control the center: Staying away from the edge gives you more room to recover after a hit.
  • Watch the opponent: Learn their jump rhythm and fire when they cannot easily dodge.
  • Keep calm: The controls are simple, so good timing is more valuable than random button presses.
  • Practice both sides: If you are playing with a friend, switch sides between matches to get comfortable with both control sets.
