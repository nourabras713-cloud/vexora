HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Player 1: WASD to move, Space to brake, C to change camera and R to restart. Player 2: Arrow keys to move, Right Shift to brake, O to change camera and P to restart.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

MX Offroad Master is a 3D off-road bike game with Mountain Ride and Free Ride modes. Choose from mountain bikes, navigate steep terrain and play alone or with another player.
