HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Player 1 controls Fireboy with the Left and Right Arrow keys to move and the Up Arrow to jump. Player 2 controls Watergirl with A and D to move and W to jump. Avoid the opposite element and green slime, collect gems, use switches and guide both characters to their matching doors.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Play Fireboy and Watergirl 1: Forest Temple, the classic two-player cooperative puzzle platform game. Guide Fireboy and Watergirl through 32 Forest Temple levels, collect red and blue diamonds, activate switches and reach their matching exits. Fireboy must avoid water, Watergirl must avoid fire, and both must stay away from green slime. Work together with a friend on one keyboard or control both characters yourself.
