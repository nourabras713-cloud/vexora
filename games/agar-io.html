HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Open the official game and choose a nickname or sign-in option if requested. On desktop, move your mouse to steer your cell, press W to eject mass and press Space to split. On touchscreens, drag to move and use the on-screen buttons for split and eject. Eat pellets and smaller cells to grow, and stay away from larger opponents.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About Agar.io

Agar.io is a multiplayer browser game created by Matheus Valadares and published by Miniclip. You control a small cell in a shared arena, collect food pellets and consume smaller players to grow. Larger cells can consume you, so positioning and timing matter. Split to chase smaller opponents or eject mass when the situation calls for it.
