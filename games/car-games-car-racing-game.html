Car Games: Car Racing Game – A 3D City Racing Challenge

Use Nitro at the Right Moment

Nitro is one of the key controls listed for the browser version. It provides a burst of speed and can help you accelerate on a suitable section of road. Using it immediately whenever it becomes available is not always the best strategy. A boost is most useful when your car is pointed in the right direction and you have enough open road to benefit from the extra speed.

Try to avoid activating nitro in the middle of a tight turn if the added speed makes steering difficult. Instead, prepare your line, complete the corner and use the boost as the road opens. This simple habit can make your driving more consistent and help you get more value from each boost.

Tips for Better Race Results

  • Learn the route first: identify the turns and longer road sections before using nitro aggressively.
  • Keep steering smooth: sudden movements can make it harder to hold a clean line.
  • Save nitro for open road: a boost is more useful when you can accelerate without immediately turning.
  • Watch the race objective: different modes may reward different approaches.
  • Use landscape orientation: this matches the game's listed orientation on mobile devices.
  • Allow the game to load: Unity WebGL games may need a little time before controls become responsive.
