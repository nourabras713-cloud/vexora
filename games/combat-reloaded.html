Combat Reloaded – Complete Online FPS Guide

Combat Reloaded is a multiplayer first-person shooter that brings classic arena combat to a web browser. Instead of exploring a large open world, players enter compact battle spaces where positioning, accuracy and quick decisions can make the difference between winning and losing a round. The game is developed by NadGames and is available through its official game page.

Fast multiplayer matches

Combat Reloaded focuses on direct player-versus-player action. Depending on the available room and current game version, players can take part in team-based matches or free-for-all battles. Team modes reward coordination: watch your teammates, cover important routes and avoid rushing into several opponents at once. In free-for-all matches, awareness becomes even more important because danger can come from any direction.

Weapons and combat strategy

A varied weapon selection gives each encounter a different rhythm. Close-range weapons are useful when moving through tight spaces, while rifles and precision weapons can be more effective when holding longer sightlines. Learn how each weapon behaves, reload when you have a safe moment and avoid standing still in exposed areas. A good position often matters as much as quick aim.

Movement and map awareness

Use movement to stay unpredictable. Move between cover, check corners before entering open areas and learn the routes that connect the main parts of each map. Sprinting can help you cross a dangerous space, but moving carefully near opponents can give you more control. If you are new to the game, spend a few rounds learning the arena layout before focusing on your score.

How to play Combat Reloaded

  1. Open the official game page using the Play Now button above.
  2. Wait for the game to load and choose an available room or match.
  3. Use the keyboard and mouse to move, aim and fire.
  4. Choose weapons that suit the map and your preferred play style.
  5. Work with your team when playing team modes and keep moving during combat.

Controls and beginner tips

The commonly listed desktop controls use WASD for movement, the mouse for aiming and shooting, Space to jump, Shift to sprint and R to reload. Some versions or modes may use additional keys. Check the in-game instructions if a control behaves differently. Beginners should start by adjusting mouse sensitivity, learning the reload timing and practicing accurate shots rather than firing continuously.
