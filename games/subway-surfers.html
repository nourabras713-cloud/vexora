HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Use the Left and Right Arrow keys to move between lanes, Up Arrow to jump and Down Arrow to roll. Press Spacebar or double-tap to activate your hoverboard. On mobile, swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to roll. Avoid trains and barriers while collecting coins and power-ups.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Subway Surfers is a colorful endless runner created by SYBO. Join Jake and his crew as they dash through busy railway tracks, dodge trains and barriers, collect coins and grab power-ups. Unlock characters and hoverboards while trying to beat your highest score. The game is playable in your browser through the official Poki listing, with no download required.
