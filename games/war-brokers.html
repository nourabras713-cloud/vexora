HOW TO PLAY

Controls

WASD: move. Mouse: aim. Left mouse: fire. Right mouse: zoom. Space: jump. C: crouch. Left Ctrl: toggle sprint. F: enter or exit vehicles and interact with objectives. R: reload. 1–7: select weapons and equipment. M: open the main menu.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About War Brokers

War Brokers is a browser-based multiplayer first-person shooter built around team combat and combined-arms action. Fight on foot, drive armored vehicles, pilot helicopters and take part in several online modes. The official website hosts the playable game directly in a modern browser.

COMPLETE GAME GUIDE

War Brokers – Complete Multiplayer FPS Guide

War Brokers is a browser-based first-person shooter that combines infantry combat with military vehicles and objective-driven multiplayer battles. Players can fight on foot, drive armored personnel carriers, operate tanks or take control of helicopters. This combined-arms design creates varied matches and makes teamwork an important part of winning.

Game Modes

The official War Brokers website lists Speed 4v4, Classic 8v8, Battle Royale and Survival. Classic matches include team-based objectives such as Team Deathmatch and capture-point battles. Other modes focus on delivering packages, launching or defending missiles, and surviving against opponents. Playlists and events can change, so check the in-game menu for the current selection.

Vehicles and Battlefield Combat

Vehicles are a defining feature. Depending on the map and mode, players can use tanks, APCs and helicopters alongside personal weapons. Vehicles provide firepower and mobility, but they also attract enemy attention. Infantry players should use cover and avoid exposing themselves to heavy vehicle weapons. Vehicle operators should coordinate with teammates and pay attention to the battlefield rather than rushing alone.

Weapons and Equipment

The game offers weapon types for different situations, including rifles, pistols, sniper weapons, grenades and heavier equipment. Primary and secondary weapon slots can be configured from the main menu. Choose a weapon that fits the distance and layout of the map. Explosives and special equipment are valuable but limited, so use them when they can support an objective or stop an enemy vehicle.

Tips for Winning More Matches

  • Play the objective: Capturing points or completing team tasks can matter more than chasing eliminations.
  • Use cover: Move between buildings, terrain and obstacles to reduce exposure.
  • Watch the minimap: Map awareness helps you anticipate threats and support teammates.
  • Choose equipment carefully: Use weapons suited to the map and save limited explosives for important moments.
  • Coordinate with your team: Vehicles are more effective when infantry players support and protect them.
  • Learn vehicle controls: Practice driving and flying before entering a contested area.
  • Adjust graphics settings: If performance is poor, reduce demanding visual options and close unnecessary browser tabs.

Browser Performance

War Brokers is designed to run in a web browser using HTML5 and Unity technology. The developers recommend a current browser, closing unnecessary tabs and using fullscreen for better performance. A keyboard and mouse are the preferred controls for the desktop FPS experience. Network quality and server availability can affect online matches.
