HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Move your mouse or drag on a touchscreen to steer your snake around the arena. On desktop, click or hold the mouse button to boost. Collect glowing orbs to grow, avoid other snakes with your head, and try to make opponents run into your body.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Slither.io is a classic multiplayer browser game that turns the simple snake concept into a competitive online arena. Start as a small snake, collect glowing orbs to grow longer and avoid crashing head-first into other players. Use careful movement, timing and positioning to survive, trap rivals and climb the leaderboard. This VEXORA page links to the original Slither.io listing on CrazyGames.
