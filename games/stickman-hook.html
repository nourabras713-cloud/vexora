HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Desktop: Hold the left mouse button or Spacebar to attach to a hook, then release to swing forward. Mobile: Tap and hold to hook, then lift your finger to release. Time each swing, use bounce pads and avoid falling before you reach the finish line.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Stickman Hook is a physics-based skill game created by Madbox. Swing through hundreds of levels by attaching to hooks, releasing at the right moment and using momentum to fly past obstacles. Simple one-button controls make it easy to start, while precise timing takes practice. Play the official browser version on Poki.
