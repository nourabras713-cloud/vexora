HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Use W, A, S, D or the arrow keys to drive. Press Spacebar for the handbrake, Shift for nitro, C to change camera, R to respawn and T to view your position and other players on the map. Controls are for keyboard play.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Madalin Stunt Cars 2 is an open-world 3D stunt driving game by Madalin Games. Choose from a large collection of sports cars and supercars, customize their colors, then explore expansive maps filled with roads, ramps, loops and stunt areas. Play alone or join an online multiplayer server to drive and perform tricks with other players.
