What Is Little Alchemy 2?

Little Alchemy 2 is the sequel to the original Little Alchemy and was developed by Recloak. It belongs to the crafting and combination puzzle genre. Rather than controlling a character through levels, you work with a collection of elements and discover what happens when two items are brought together. Each successful recipe adds a new discovery to your encyclopedia, giving you more ingredients to use in later experiments.

The appeal comes from curiosity. Some combinations feel logical, while others are playful surprises. You can create basic materials and natural phenomena, then use those discoveries to work toward tools, living things, places and more complex ideas. There is no need to rush: the experience focuses on exploration, testing possibilities and gradually expanding your collection.

How to Play Little Alchemy 2

When the game opens, you will see your available elements and a workspace. Select an item and drag it onto another item. If the two elements can form a valid recipe, the game creates a new discovery. The new item is then available for future combinations. You can repeat the process with other elements and use the encyclopedia to review what you have already found.

  1. Start with the basics: locate earth, air, fire and water in your starting collection.
  2. Drag an element: move one item into the central workspace.
  3. Combine two items: drop one element onto another to test a recipe.
  4. Save discoveries: new creations are added to your encyclopedia.
  5. Experiment again: use each new item with other elements to unlock more possibilities.
  6. Organize the board: use the clean-up control when the workspace becomes crowded.
