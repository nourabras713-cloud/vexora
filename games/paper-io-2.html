HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen joystick or drag control to steer your character. On a computer, use WASD, the Arrow Keys or your mouse if available. Leave your territory to draw a trail, then return to your colored area to close the loop and claim the enclosed space. Avoid opponents while your trail is exposed, and try to cross an opponent's trail to eliminate them.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About Paper.io 2

Paper.io 2 is a territory-capture .io game created by Voodoo. Move around the arena and leave a trail to claim new areas by connecting back to your existing territory. Your trail is vulnerable while you are outside your safe zone, so watch for rivals trying to cross it. Expand carefully, cut off opponents when they expose their trails and aim to control as much of the map as possible.
