HOW TO PLAY

Controls

W / A / S / D: move. Mouse: look and aim. Left mouse button: shoot. Right mouse button: aim down sights. Space: jump. Shift: crouch. E: dash. R: reload. 1 / 2 / 3: switch weapons. Enter: chat. Tab: view scores. Controls can be changed in Settings.

Kirka.io – Free Voxel Multiplayer FPS

Kirka.io is a browser-based first-person shooter that places players in a colorful voxel world. Its block-style maps and characters give the game a distinctive look, while the core experience focuses on aiming, movement, weapon choice and competing against other players. You can open the official website in a modern browser and enter multiplayer action without installing a separate desktop game.

Pick a mode and join the fight

Kirka.io offers several styles of play. Team Deathmatch is built around two teams competing for eliminations, so staying near teammates and supporting their fights can make a difference. Free-for-All turns every player into an opponent and rewards awareness in all directions. Objective modes shift attention from chasing eliminations to controlling a point or completing a team goal. Parkour provides a change of pace by focusing on movement and timed routes rather than ordinary gunfights. The available mode rotation and server population can vary over time.

Weapons and movement

Before a match, players can choose from available weapon slots and prepare a loadout. Different weapons suit different distances and situations. A fast-firing weapon can help in close encounters, while a more precise rifle is useful when holding a longer sightline. Learn the reload timing and avoid exposing yourself while changing magazines. Movement is also important: jumping, crouching and the dash ability can help you reposition, escape danger or reach cover. Use these actions deliberately rather than moving without a plan.

Clans, progression and customization

The Kirka community includes clans and competitive activities. Players can build up their profile through match participation, earn cosmetic rewards and take part in clan competition. Customization options let players adjust settings such as mouse sensitivity, scope sensitivity, crosshair appearance, field of view and key bindings. Spend a moment tuning these controls so aiming and movement feel comfortable on your device.

Tips for new players

  • Start with a familiar mode such as Team Deathmatch to learn the maps and weapon handling.
  • Use cover and avoid standing still in open lanes.
  • Keep teammates in sight during team matches instead of taking every fight alone.
  • Use the dash to reach cover or change position, not simply whenever it becomes available.
  • Adjust mouse sensitivity and crosshair settings gradually until aiming feels consistent.
  • Try Parkour to practise movement and learn map layouts without the pressure of a firefight.

Is Kirka.io a browser game?
Yes. Kirka.io is designed to run in a web browser, with no separate game download required.

Is Kirka.io multiplayer?

Can I play Kirka.io with friends?
