Best Drift Boss Tips for Beginners

  • Look ahead, not down: focus on the upcoming corner rather than the car itself.
  • Turn before the bend: begin your input slightly before reaching the corner so the car has time to change direction.
  • Use short corrections: small, controlled holds are often safer than holding for too long.
  • Stay centered: leave enough room on both sides to handle the next turn.
  • Learn the rhythm: many sections alternate between left and right, so practice recognizing the pattern.
  • Do not chase every coin: a risky coin is not worth losing a strong run.
  • Practice consistently: short attempts help you learn how early to hold and when to release.
  • Stay calm at higher speed: panic inputs often cause oversteering and missed corners.

How to Get a Higher Score in Drift Boss
