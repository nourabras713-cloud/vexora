HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Single player: use W, A, S, D or the arrow keys to drive, Shift for nitro, R to reset your car, T to look back and C to change camera. Two-player mode: Player 1 uses the arrow keys, M for nitro, O to reset, L to look back and K for camera. Player 2 uses W, A, S, D, Left Shift, R, T and C.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Night City Racing is a 3D supercar driving game developed by RHM Interactive. Choose from 10 sports cars, customize performance and appearance, and play 16 races, five special challenges or a free-drive mode. Race against computer opponents or share the keyboard with a friend in local split-screen mode. Earn in-game cash to unlock cars and upgrade engine, brakes, agility, turbo, rims and paint.
