Tips to Improve Your Performance

  • Use cover: Move from one protected position to another instead of crossing open spaces without a plan.
  • Keep moving: Change direction and avoid standing still while aiming for a target.
  • Control your aim: Short, deliberate bursts can be more effective than holding the trigger continuously at long range.
  • Reload between fights: Check your ammunition after an encounter and reload somewhere safe.
  • Learn each map: Remember common routes, entrances and elevated positions to predict where opponents may appear.
  • Choose the right weapon: Adapt your loadout to the distance and layout of the map.
  • Play the objective: In team modes, capturing a zone or supporting teammates can contribute more than chasing eliminations.
  • Communicate carefully: Use team communication when available, but avoid sharing personal information in public chats.
