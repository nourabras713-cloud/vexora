HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Move and jump with WASD or the Arrow Keys. Press Space to attack with your primary weapon and V for your secondary weapon. Press C or Q to switch weapons. Use E, R, T, Y and U for item slots. Press I for inventory, P for professions, M for the map, L for friends, and Enter to chat.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About GoBattle.io

GoBattle.io is a free multiplayer pixel-art action RPG set in the kingdom of Valdoran. Explore dungeons, fight monsters and bosses, develop your hero, and meet other players in several competitive modes. You can enter King Mode, Battle Royale, Damageball or ranked 1v1 PvP, or team up with friends for dungeon adventures. The game runs in a web browser on desktop and mobile, with no download required.

COMPLETE GAME GUIDE

GoBattle.io – Complete Multiplayer RPG Guide

GoBattle.io is a browser-based multiplayer action RPG set in the fantasy kingdom of Valdoran. It combines dungeon exploration, character progression and player-versus-player combat in a pixel-art world. You can explore dangerous areas, fight monsters and bosses, collect equipment and test your skills against other players.

Explore dungeons and defeat bosses

Adventure Mode takes your hero through dungeons filled with enemies, hazards and powerful bosses. Prepare before entering, watch enemy attack patterns and use your equipment carefully. Exploring different environments helps you discover new challenges and develop your character.

Choose a multiplayer mode

GoBattle.io offers several ways to compete. King Mode is a free-for-all challenge where players collect coins and try to become the king of the server. Battle Royale focuses on survival: stay alive while opponents are eliminated. Damageball is a team-based mode that combines combat with a ball objective. Ranked 1v1 PvP matches you against another player and contributes to seasonal rankings.

Build and improve your hero

Character development is an important part of the game. The official guide describes attributes including Attack, Defense, Luck, maximum Health, Regeneration and Speed. Consider how these stats support your preferred approach: a defensive setup can help you survive longer, while attack and speed can support a more aggressive style.

Play with friends

You can add friends using their player IDs and invite them to private matches. The game also supports grouping for dungeon adventures. Coordinate your roles, communicate through chat and choose match settings that suit your group.

Controls and beginner tips

Use WASD or the Arrow Keys to move. Space attacks with your primary weapon, while V uses the secondary weapon. C or Q switches weapons. The item slots and menus provide access to inventory, professions, map and friends. Learn the controls in a quieter area before entering competitive matches.

  • Explore carefully and learn enemy patterns before rushing into a dungeon.
  • Keep an eye on health and equipment during longer fights.
  • Use the mode that matches your goal: exploration, survival, team play or ranked duels.
  • Invite friends for private matches or cooperative dungeon runs.
