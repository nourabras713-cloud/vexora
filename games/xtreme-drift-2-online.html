Xtreme Drift 2 Online Controls

Use Up, Left, Down and Right to drive. Spacebar activates the handbrake, C changes the camera, F uses turbo, G slows time and L controls the lights.

XTREME DRIFT 2 GUIDE

Xtreme Drift 2 Online – Drift & Racing Guide

Xtreme Drift 2 Online expands the classic drift formula with multiplayer competition. You can race other players, practise drifting alone, explore free driving and create or join online lobbies.

Successful drifting requires smooth steering and throttle control. Use the handbrake to initiate or adjust a slide, then counter-steer to keep the car on the desired line. Turbo is most useful when the car is already stable and pointing in the right direction.

With multiple tracks, cars and upgrades, Xtreme Drift 2 Online is a strong choice for players searching for browser drifting and multiplayer car racing.
