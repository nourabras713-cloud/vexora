HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Use the Up Arrow to accelerate, Left and Right Arrows to balance your bike, and Down Arrow to brake. Perform flips when it is safe and avoid crashing.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Xtreme Drift 2 Online is a fast-paced motorcycle racing game built around timing, balance and precision. Ride through obstacle-filled tracks, launch over ramps, avoid traps and perform flips to improve your time.
