HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Move your monkey with the controls shown in the game. Stand near crops to harvest them, carry produce to the matching shelves and wait at the checkout to collect customers' payments. Reinvest your earnings in upgrades, new products and helpers to expand the supermarket.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About Monkey Mart

Monkey Mart is a supermarket management game created by TinyDobbins. Guide your monkey through the store, harvest crops, carry produce to shelves and collect payments at the checkout. As the market grows, unlock more products, hire monkey workers and upgrade the shop to keep customers moving. This VEXORA page links to the browser-playable version on Poki.
