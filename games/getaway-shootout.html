HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Player 1: W jumps left, E jumps right, and R uses a weapon or power-up. Player 2: I jumps left, O jumps right, and P uses a weapon or power-up. You move by jumping rather than walking. On supported touch devices, use the on-screen controls.

COMPLETE GAME GUIDE

Getaway Shootout – The Complete Guide to the Physics-Based Action Game

Getaway Shootout is a fast, unpredictable action game created by New Eich Games. Its central challenge is unusual: your character does not move with normal walking controls. Instead, you jump left or right to cross the stage, avoid hazards and race three opponents toward the getaway point. Weapons and power-ups appear along the route, giving you ways to interrupt rivals or gain an advantage. The simple controls are easy to understand, but the physics make every jump a small challenge.

What Is Getaway Shootout?

Getaway Shootout combines platform racing, shooting and ragdoll-style physics. Each round places several characters on a course filled with gaps, platforms and obstacles. Your goal is to reach the extraction point before the other competitors. You can play alone against computer-controlled rivals or choose the local two-player mode and compete with a friend on the same keyboard.

The game was developed by New Eich Games, the studio also known for Rooftop Snipers and House of Hazards. Its pixel-art presentation and exaggerated movement give the matches a playful style. Different stages and character choices help keep repeated rounds varied, while weapons and power-ups add another layer of competition.

How to Play and Reach the Getaway

At the start of a round, focus on the route ahead rather than pressing every key at once. Hold the left or right jump control to prepare the direction, then release to make your character leap. Because movement is based on jumps, the timing and length of each input affect how you cross platforms. Watch the next landing area and adjust your direction before you reach the edge.

Collect weapons and useful items when they appear, but do not let every pickup distract you from the finish. A weapon can slow an opponent or create an opening, yet a detour may cost valuable time. The best decision depends on where the item lands, how close the rivals are and whether the route ahead is safe.

Weapons, Items and Character Choices

Weapons and power-ups are scattered across the stages. Picking one up can help you disrupt a rival, defend your position or move through a difficult section. Learn what an item does before relying on it, and remember that using an item at the wrong moment may leave you exposed. The game also includes a selection of characters that players can unlock, adding cosmetic variety as they earn in-game rewards.

Tips for Better Getaway Shootout Runs

  • Learn the jump rhythm: Practice short left and right jumps before attempting difficult gaps.
  • Look ahead: Aim for a safe landing spot instead of reacting only when your character reaches an obstacle.
  • Do not overcorrect: Rapidly alternating directions can make your movement unpredictable. Use deliberate inputs.
  • Use weapons strategically: Pick up items when they are on your route and use them when a rival is within a useful range.
  • Keep moving toward the exit: The goal is to reach the getaway point first, not to spend the entire round fighting.
  • Practice each map: Remember where gaps and platforms appear so you can plan jumps earlier.
  • In two-player mode, watch your rival: Time your jumps and item use around the other player's position.
