HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Use the Left and Right Arrow keys or A and D to steer the ball. Keep the ball on the track, avoid red obstacles and do not fall off the edges. The ball moves forward automatically and gets faster as you survive.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Slope is a fast-paced 3D endless runner where you steer a rolling ball down a neon track filled with sharp turns, gaps and red obstacles. The ball accelerates as you survive, so every second demands quicker reactions and precise movement. Chase a higher score, beat your personal best and see how far you can travel.
