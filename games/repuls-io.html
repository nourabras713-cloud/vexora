Repuls.io – Complete Sci-Fi FPS Guide

Repuls.io is a science-fiction multiplayer first-person shooter built for browser play. Developed by docski, it takes the familiar arena-shooter format and expands it with a broader battlefield, futuristic equipment and vehicles. Players can move through large maps, fight on foot, use different weapons and make use of vehicles and aircraft where available.

A different kind of browser FPS

Many browser shooters focus on compact arenas and short infantry-only encounters. Repuls.io adds more variety to the battlefield. Its official game description highlights vehicles, aircraft and equipment alongside real-time multiplayer combat. That mix gives players more than one way to approach an encounter: move with the team, take advantage of the map, or use available equipment to change the pace of a fight.

Weapons and equipment

The official game page lists a broad arsenal that includes miniguns, rocket launchers and sniper rifles, as well as grappling abilities. Different weapons suit different situations. A precision weapon can help at longer distances, while heavier weapons can be useful when pressure builds around an objective. Learn how each item works and switch equipment to match the distance and situation rather than relying on one option.

Vehicles and aircraft

Vehicle combat is one of Repuls.io's distinguishing features. The official site describes driving vehicles and flying aircraft with teammates. These options can change how a team moves across a map and how players approach open areas. When using a vehicle, pay attention to the surrounding battlefield and coordinate with teammates instead of treating it as a replacement for map awareness.

Movement and map awareness

Movement is central to surviving in a multiplayer FPS. Use cover, avoid staying exposed for too long and learn the routes between important areas. The grappling ability can add vertical movement and help reposition, while jumping and melee options provide additional ways to respond at close range. Knowing when to reload, change weapons or use an item can be just as important as aiming accurately.
