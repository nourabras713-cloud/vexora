HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

On desktop, use the Up Arrow to jump, Down Arrow to slide, and Left or Right Arrow to turn and change lanes. On a phone or tablet, swipe up to jump, down to slide, and left or right to turn or move between lanes. Collect coins, avoid obstacles and keep running as the speed increases.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About Temple Run 2

Temple Run 2 is an endless runner developed by Imangi Studios. After grabbing a golden idol, your explorer races through ancient ruins while a demon monkey chases close behind. React quickly to jump over gaps and fire traps, slide under low barriers, turn at sharp corners and switch lanes to collect coins. The browser version is available on Poki and supports desktop, phone and tablet play.
