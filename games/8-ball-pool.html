HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Use your mouse or touch to aim and interact with the pool table. Pocket all balls in your group, then sink the eight ball to win. Avoid pocketing the eight ball too early.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

8 Ball Pool brings the classic billiards challenge to your browser. Aim carefully, plan your shots and pocket the balls in your assigned group before sinking the black eight ball. The CrazyGames listing supports desktop and mobile browsers. Choose Play Now to continue to the original game page.
