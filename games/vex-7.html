HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Use A/D or the Left and Right Arrow keys to move. Press W or the Up Arrow to jump and climb. Press S or the Down Arrow to crouch or slide. On mobile, use the on-screen controls. Reach checkpoints, avoid hazards and make it to the exit.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Vex 7 is a fast-paced motorcycle racing game built around timing, balance and precision. Ride through obstacle-filled tracks, launch over ramps, avoid traps and perform flips to improve your time. This VEXORA page is a directory listing that sends players to the original CrazyGames game page.
