Frequently asked questions

Is Eggy Car a browser game?
Yes. The official game is available directly through its website in a supported browser.

Is Eggy Car multiplayer?
Yes. Eggy Car is a real-time multiplayer FPS with solo and team-based match types.

What modes can I play?
Common modes include Free For All, Team Deathmatch, Gun Game and Hardpoint. The available rotation may change.

What are the basic controls?
WASD moves your character, the mouse aims and fires, Space jumps, R reloads and number keys switch weapons. Check the in-game settings for the current movement bindings.

Eggy Car has mobile availability, but a computer with keyboard and mouse is recommended for more precise FPS controls.
