HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Use the arrow keys or WASD to accelerate, brake and steer. H activates the horn, Y performs a wheelie, T and U look left and right, R shifts up, F shifts down, and P pauses the game.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Traffic Rider is a first-person motorcycle racing game by SKGames. Ride through highways filled with traffic, complete career missions, earn rewards and upgrade different motorcycles while chasing faster times and longer rides.
