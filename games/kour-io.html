Controls

W / A / S / D: move. Mouse: aim and shoot. Left mouse button: fire. Right mouse button: aim. Space: jump. Shift: slide or crouch depending on the current control settings. R: reload. 1 / 2 / 3: switch weapons. Check the in-game controls menu for the latest bindings.

Kour.io – Free Multiplayer Browser FPS

Kour.io is a fast-paced first-person shooter that brings multiplayer arena combat directly to a web browser. Developed by LEGiON Platforms, the game uses a colorful low-poly visual style and focuses on movement, quick aiming and short competitive matches. Players can open the official website, choose a class and enter an online battle without installing a conventional desktop game.

Choose a game mode

Kour.io offers several match styles for different approaches to combat. Free For All is a solo competition where every other player is an opponent. Team Deathmatch divides players into teams and rewards coordinated eliminations. Gun Game changes the weapon challenge as players progress through the match, encouraging adaptability with different firearms. Hardpoint shifts the focus to controlling a marked area and holding it against the opposing team. Mode availability can change as the developers rotate or update the game.

Classes and weapons

Class selection lets players choose a combat role that matches their preferred style. Some classes are suited to direct rifle engagements, while others offer different weapons or abilities that can influence how you approach a map. Experiment with the available options to discover whether you prefer close-range pressure, accurate shots from a distance or a more mobile style. Weapon handling, reload timing and choosing when to engage are important parts of every match.

Movement makes a difference

Good movement is a major part of Kour.io. Use jumps and the movement actions shown in the current settings to avoid predictable paths, cross open areas and reposition during a fight. Learn the layout of each arena so you know where opponents may appear and where you can find cover. Avoid standing still while aiming or reloading, and try to approach contested areas from a route that gives you more than one option to retreat.

Tips for new players

  • Begin with Free For All or Team Deathmatch to learn aiming and weapon behavior.
  • Choose one class and practise with it before frequently changing your loadout.
  • Keep moving, but use cover instead of jumping into every open fight.
  • Reload when you have enough distance or cover to do it safely.
  • Watch the minimap and listen for nearby combat to anticipate enemy positions.
  • Adjust mouse sensitivity and graphics settings to suit your computer.
