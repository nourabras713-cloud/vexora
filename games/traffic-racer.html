HOW TO PLAY

Game instructions

Use the arrow keys to drive and steer. On mobile or tablet, use the on-screen touch controls for steering, acceleration and braking. Avoid collisions, collect coins and keep driving to increase your distance.

ABOUT THIS GAME

About the game

Traffic Racer is an endless highway driving game focused on speed, quick reactions and precise lane changes. Drive through busy multi-lane roads, avoid cars and trucks, collect coins and work toward longer runs. Coins and rewards help you unlock vehicles and improve your garage. Traffic becomes more demanding as you continue, so planning your moves is just as important as driving fast.
